A bit about me

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science graduate (2026) who likes building the whole thing: the database, the REST API, and the React screen on top of it.

I build RESTful APIs with FastAPI, SQLAlchemy and Pydantic, lock them down with JWT authentication, and wire them into React front ends. Right now I'm interning at A2R Software Consulting, shipping database-backed APIs across Python, Node.js/Express and React.
